Equilibrium

Hold the marker in the centre against the wind.

About Equilibrium

Equilibrium is a balance game. A marker slides along a track while gusts of wind push it left and right. Hold the left or right button to push back and keep it near the centre until the timer ends. Falling off either edge ends the run.

How to play

  1. Hold the left or right button to push the marker.
  2. Release to let it drift.
  3. Keep the marker on the track until time runs out.

Tips and strategy

  • Use short presses, not long holds.
  • React to the marker speed, not just its position.
  • Stay near the centre to earn more stars.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Equilibrium?
Hold the left or right button to push the marker. Release to let it drift. Keep the marker on the track until time runs out.
What is a good strategy for Equilibrium?
Use short presses, not long holds.
How many levels does Equilibrium have?
Equilibrium has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Equilibrium is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
