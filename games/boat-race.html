Boat Race

Row in rhythm, left then right, to beat the rival boat.

About Boat Race

Boat Race is inspired by the traditional boat races on the rivers of Bengal. Rowers must keep a steady rhythm, alternating left and right strokes. Tap in rhythm to push your boat forward and cross the finish line before the rival does.

How to play

  1. Tap Left and Right alternately to row.
  2. Tapping the same side twice breaks your rhythm and slows the boat.
  3. Cross the finish line before the rival boat.

Tips and strategy

  • Find a steady beat and keep it.
  • Use two thumbs on a phone, one for each side.
  • The left and right arrow keys also work on a computer.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Boat Race?
Tap Left and Right alternately to row. Tapping the same side twice breaks your rhythm and slows the boat. Cross the finish line before the rival boat.
What is a good strategy for Boat Race?
Find a steady beat and keep it.
How many levels does Boat Race have?
Boat Race has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Boat Race is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
