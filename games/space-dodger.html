Space Dodger

Steer your ship and dodge the falling rocks.

About Space Dodger

Space Dodger is a survival arcade game. Asteroids rain down the screen and you slide your ship left and right to avoid them. Rocks fall faster and more often as levels progress, and you must survive for the full countdown.

How to play

  1. Drag across the board or press the arrow keys to move your ship.
  2. Avoid every falling rock. Each hit costs a life.
  3. Survive until the timer reaches zero.

Tips and strategy

  • Watch the top of the screen, not your ship.
  • Stay near the centre so you can dodge either way.
  • Small, steady moves beat big panicked swings.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Space Dodger?
Drag across the board or press the arrow keys to move your ship. Avoid every falling rock. Each hit costs a life. Survive until the timer reaches zero.
What is a good strategy for Space Dodger?
Watch the top of the screen, not your ship.
How many levels does Space Dodger have?
Space Dodger has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Space Dodger is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
