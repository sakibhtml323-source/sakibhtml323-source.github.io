Ludo Classic

Race your tokens round the board and capture the rival.

About Ludo Classic

Ludo Classic is a compact version of the beloved family board game. Roll the die and race two tokens around the track to the finish while the rival does the same. Land on a rival token to send it back to the start, and roll a six for another turn. Ringed start squares keep tokens protected.

How to play

  1. Press Roll, then move one of your tokens by the number rolled.
  2. Land exactly on a rival token to send it back to its start.
  3. Get both tokens home first. You need an exact roll to finish.

Tips and strategy

  • Capture whenever you can, but check what the rival can capture next.
  • Do not waste a big roll on a token that is about to finish.
  • Squares marked with a ring cannot be captured.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How many levels does Ludo Classic have?
Ludo Classic has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Ludo Classic is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
