Whack-a-Mole

Whack the moles before they dive back underground.

About Whack-a-Mole

Whack-a-Mole is a quick reaction game on a three-by-three grid. Moles pop up for a short time and you tap them before they hide. At higher levels several moles appear at once and each one stays for less time.

How to play

  1. Tap a mole while it is above ground.
  2. A mole that escapes counts as a miss.
  3. Reach the target score before three moles get away.

Tips and strategy

  • Watch the whole grid, not a single hole.
  • Tap the mole that has been up the longest first.
  • Use light, quick taps and keep your hand relaxed.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Whack-a-Mole?
Tap a mole while it is above ground. A mole that escapes counts as a miss. Reach the target score before three moles get away.
What is a good strategy for Whack-a-Mole?
Watch the whole grid, not a single hole.
How many levels does Whack-a-Mole have?
Whack-a-Mole has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Whack-a-Mole is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
