Snake Reborn

Guide the snake to the apples without crashing.

About Snake Reborn

Snake Reborn brings the classic arcade game back with 150 levels. Steer the snake to eat apples, growing longer each time. Higher levels run faster and add obstacles to the board. Crash into a wall, an obstacle or your own tail and the run ends.

How to play

  1. Swipe, use the arrow keys or tap the arrow buttons to steer.
  2. Eat the gold apples to grow.
  3. Reach the apple target without crashing.

Tips and strategy

  • Keep to the edges and leave open space in the middle.
  • Plan your turns two moves ahead.
  • Avoid trapping yourself in a loop as your tail gets longer.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Snake Reborn?
Swipe, use the arrow keys or tap the arrow buttons to steer. Eat the gold apples to grow. Reach the apple target without crashing.
What is a good strategy for Snake Reborn?
Keep to the edges and leave open space in the middle.
How many levels does Snake Reborn have?
Snake Reborn has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Snake Reborn is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
