Sudoku Mini

Fill the 4x4 grid so every row, column and box holds 1 to 4.

About Sudoku Mini

Sudoku Mini is a compact version of the classic number puzzle. A four-by-four grid must be filled so that each row, column and two-by-two box contains the digits 1 to 4 exactly once. It is a gentle introduction to logical deduction, and the number of blanks grows with each level.

How to play

  1. Tap an empty cell, then tap a number below the grid.
  2. Every row, column and 2x2 box must contain 1, 2, 3 and 4 once each.
  3. Conflicting numbers turn red. Fewer mistakes earn more stars.

Tips and strategy

  • Start with the row, column or box that already has the most numbers.
  • For each empty cell, list which digits are still missing from its row, column and box.
  • If a digit can only fit in one cell of a row, place it there.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How many levels does Sudoku Mini have?
Sudoku Mini has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Sudoku Mini is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
