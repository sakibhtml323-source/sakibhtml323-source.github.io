Pipe Connect

Rotate the pipes to connect the left edge to the right edge.

About Pipe Connect

Pipe Connect is a rotation puzzle. A hidden channel runs from the gold edge on the left to the right side of the board, but every pipe has been turned the wrong way. Tap pipes to rotate them until water flows all the way across. Larger boards and more decoy pipes appear as levels advance.

How to play

  1. Tap a pipe to rotate it 90 degrees clockwise.
  2. Water enters from the gold edge on the left and lights up pipes as it flows.
  3. Connect it all the way to the right edge.

Tips and strategy

  • Start at the gold edge and fix one pipe at a time.
  • Straight pipes only have two states: horizontal or vertical.
  • Pipes that are already lit are on the right track.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Pipe Connect?
Tap a pipe to rotate it 90 degrees clockwise. Water enters from the gold edge on the left and lights up pipes as it flows. Connect it all the way to the right edge.
What is a good strategy for Pipe Connect?
Start at the gold edge and fix one pipe at a time.
How many levels does Pipe Connect have?
Pipe Connect has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Pipe Connect is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
