Odd One Out

Spot the one tile whose shade is slightly different.

About Odd One Out

Odd One Out is a colour-perception challenge. A grid of near-identical tiles hides one with a slightly different shade, and you have to find it. Grids grow larger and the difference shrinks as levels rise, training visual discrimination and focus.

How to play

  1. Look across the grid for the tile with a different shade.
  2. Tap it to move to the next round.
  3. A wrong tap costs one of your three lives.

Tips and strategy

  • Relax your eyes and look at the whole grid at once instead of tile by tile.
  • Tilt your screen brightness up if differences look too small.
  • Take your time: accuracy decides your stars.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Odd One Out?
Look across the grid for the tile with a different shade. Tap it to move to the next round. A wrong tap costs one of your three lives.
What is a good strategy for Odd One Out?
Relax your eyes and look at the whole grid at once instead of tile by tile.
How many levels does Odd One Out have?
Odd One Out has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Odd One Out is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
