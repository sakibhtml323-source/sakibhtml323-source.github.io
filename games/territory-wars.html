Territory Wars

Capture the board by flanking and flipping rival discs.

About Territory Wars

Territory Wars is a strategy game in the Reversi family. Place a disc so that it traps one or more rival discs in a straight line, and those discs flip to your colour. The player with more discs when the game ends wins. Early levels use a 6x6 board, later ones the full 8x8 against a deeper-thinking rival.

How to play

  1. Tap a highlighted square to place a disc.
  2. Rival discs trapped between your discs in a line flip to your colour.
  3. When the board is full or nobody can move, the player with more discs wins.

Tips and strategy

  • Corners can never be flipped, so take them whenever you can.
  • Avoid squares next to corners early on because they give the corner away.
  • Having fewer discs in the middle game is often better: mobility matters more.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Territory Wars?
Tap a highlighted square to place a disc. Rival discs trapped between your discs in a line flip to your colour. When the board is full or nobody can move, the player with more discs wins.
What is a good strategy for Territory Wars?
Corners can never be flipped, so take them whenever you can.
How many levels does Territory Wars have?
Territory Wars has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Territory Wars is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
