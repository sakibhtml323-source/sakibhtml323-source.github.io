Dash Gate

Dash through each gate while it is open.

About Dash Gate

Dash Gate is a row of gates that open and close at different speeds. Your runner can only pass the highlighted gate, and only while it glows green. Press Dash at the right moment to pass it and move on to the next. A bad dash costs a life.

How to play

  1. Watch the highlighted gate.
  2. Press Dash while it is green.
  3. Pass every gate before you lose three lives.

Tips and strategy

  • Learn the rhythm of the gate before you dash.
  • Do not try to beat the closing edge.
  • Gates further along keep cycling, so focus only on the current one.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Dash Gate?
Watch the highlighted gate. Press Dash while it is green. Pass every gate before you lose three lives.
What is a good strategy for Dash Gate?
Learn the rhythm of the gate before you dash.
How many levels does Dash Gate have?
Dash Gate has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Dash Gate is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
