Tile Swap

Swap tiles to sort the numbers in the fewest moves.

About Tile Swap

Tile Swap is a sorting puzzle. Numbered tiles are shuffled across a grid and you swap any two of them to put them in order. Because any pair can be swapped, planning the shortest route matters more than dexterity.

How to play

  1. Tap one tile, then another, to swap them.
  2. Arrange the numbers from smallest to largest, left to right and top to bottom.
  3. Use no more swaps than the par shown to earn three stars.

Tips and strategy

  • Send each number straight to its correct spot instead of shuffling around it.
  • Look for two tiles that belong in each other's places: one swap fixes both.
  • Count how many tiles are out of place to estimate the par.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

How many levels does Tile Swap have?
Tile Swap has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Tile Swap is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
