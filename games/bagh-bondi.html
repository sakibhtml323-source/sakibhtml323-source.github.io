Bagh Bondi

Trap the tiger before it escapes the forest.

About Bagh Bondi

Bagh Bondi takes its name from a traditional Bengali game of tigers and goats. A tiger prowls a forest grid and tries to reach the edge. Each turn you place a blocker on an empty square, and then the tiger moves towards the nearest exit. Surround the tiger so it has nowhere left to go.

How to play

  1. Tap an empty square to place a blocker there.
  2. After your move the tiger steps towards the nearest edge.
  3. Trap it completely before it reaches the edge of the board.

Tips and strategy

  • Block the square the tiger wants to step on next.
  • Think two moves ahead: the tiger always takes the shortest path.
  • Close the gaps nearest the tiger first, then the far ones.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good strategy for Bagh Bondi?

How many levels does Bagh Bondi have?
Bagh Bondi has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Bagh Bondi is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
