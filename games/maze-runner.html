Maze Runner

Find the shortest way from the start to the exit.

About Maze Runner

Maze Runner drops you into a freshly generated maze and asks you to reach the exit. Mazes grow from a compact 6x6 to a sprawling 13x13. Every level is generated from a seed, so each one is unique but fully repeatable.

How to play

  1. Swipe, use the arrow keys or tap the arrow buttons to move.
  2. Walls block your way. Find a path to the glowing exit.
  3. Fewer moves earn more stars.

Tips and strategy

  • Follow one wall with your hand and you will always find the exit.
  • Avoid walking back into dead ends you have already explored.
  • Look for corridors that lead towards the exit corner.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Maze Runner?
Swipe, use the arrow keys or tap the arrow buttons to move. Walls block your way. Find a path to the glowing exit. Fewer moves earn more stars.
What is a good strategy for Maze Runner?
Follow one wall with your hand and you will always find the exit.
How many levels does Maze Runner have?
Maze Runner has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Maze Runner is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
