Kite Fight

Time your cut to snap the rival kite string.

About Kite Fight

Kite Fight is inspired by the kite-flying contests of Bengal. Two kites tangle in the sky and each tries to cut the other string. Watch the marker slide across the bar and press Cut when it is inside the gold zone to sever the rival string before yours frays.

How to play

  1. Watch the marker move back and forth along the bar.
  2. Press Cut when the marker is inside the gold zone.
  3. Each clean cut weakens the rival string. Each miss frays yours.

Tips and strategy

  • Anticipate the marker and press just before it reaches the zone.
  • The zone shrinks on higher levels, so stay patient.
  • Do not tap randomly: misses cost more than hits gain.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How many levels does Kite Fight have?
Kite Fight has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Kite Fight is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
