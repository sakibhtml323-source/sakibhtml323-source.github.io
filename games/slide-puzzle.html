Slide Puzzle

Slide numbered tiles back into order using the single empty space.

About Slide Puzzle

Slide Puzzle (the classic 8- and 15-puzzle) asks you to restore numbered tiles to order using one empty space. It rewards spatial planning: solve a row, lock it in, then work on the rest. Early levels use a 3x3 grid and harder levels switch to 4x4.

How to play

  1. Tap a tile next to the empty space to slide it across.
  2. Arrange tiles from 1 upward, left to right and top to bottom, with the gap in the bottom-right corner.
  3. Solve it in fewer moves to earn three stars.

Tips and strategy

  • Solve the top row first, then the left column, and treat what remains as a smaller puzzle.
  • Avoid undoing your last move; it only wastes turns.
  • Look two or three moves ahead before you slide.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How many levels does Slide Puzzle have?
Slide Puzzle has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Slide Puzzle is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
