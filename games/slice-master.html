Slice Master

Slice the flying fruit and avoid the bombs.

About Slice Master

Slice Master launches colourful fruit into the air and challenges you to slice them with a tap before they fall. Bombs are mixed in, and touching one costs a life. Fruit flies faster and bombs appear more often as levels advance, and six escaped fruit end the run.

How to play

  1. Tap fruit while it is in the air to slice it.
  2. Never tap a bomb. Each one costs a life.
  3. Slice enough fruit before six escape.

Tips and strategy

  • Slice fruit near the top of its arc when it moves slowest.
  • Check for bombs before you tap in a crowd.
  • Stay calm during busy moments: one careful tap beats three rushed ones.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Slice Master?
Tap fruit while it is in the air to slice it. Never tap a bomb. Each one costs a life. Slice enough fruit before six escape.
What is a good strategy for Slice Master?
Slice fruit near the top of its arc when it moves slowest.
How many levels does Slice Master have?
Slice Master has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Slice Master is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
