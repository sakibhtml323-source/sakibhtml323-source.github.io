Rail Switch

Throw the switch to send each train to its station.

About Rail Switch

Rail Switch is a signal-box game. Gold and purple trains roll down a single track towards a junction, and you control a switch that sends them left to station A or right to station B. Gold trains belong at A and purple trains at B. Deliver them correctly and keep the timetable.

How to play

  1. Watch the colour of the train heading for the junction.
  2. Tap Switch track to set the route: gold to A, purple to B.
  3. Deliver enough trains correctly before three go to the wrong station.

Tips and strategy

  • Set the switch for the next train as soon as the previous one has passed.
  • Only the route at the moment a train reaches the junction counts.
  • On later levels trains arrive quicker, so plan ahead.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Rail Switch?
Watch the colour of the train heading for the junction. Tap Switch track to set the route: gold to A, purple to B. Deliver enough trains correctly before three go to the wrong station.
What is a good strategy for Rail Switch?

How many levels does Rail Switch have?
Rail Switch has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Rail Switch is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
