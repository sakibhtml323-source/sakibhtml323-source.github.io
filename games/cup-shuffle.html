Cup Shuffle

Follow the ball as the cups shuffle, then pick the right one.

About Cup Shuffle

Cup Shuffle is a classic tracking game. A ball is hidden under one cup, the cups swap places faster and faster, and you pick where the ball ended up. It trains sustained attention and smooth visual tracking.

How to play

  1. Watch which cup hides the ball.
  2. Follow it as the cups swap places.
  3. Find the ball three times before you lose three lives.

Tips and strategy

  • Fix your eyes on the cup with the ball and ignore the others.
  • Do not try to count swaps.
  • Four cups appear from level 76, so stay sharp.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Cup Shuffle?
Watch which cup hides the ball. Follow it as the cups swap places. Find the ball three times before you lose three lives.
What is a good strategy for Cup Shuffle?
Fix your eyes on the cup with the ball and ignore the others.
How many levels does Cup Shuffle have?
Cup Shuffle has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Cup Shuffle is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
