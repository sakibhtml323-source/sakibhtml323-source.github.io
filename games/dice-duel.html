Dice Duel

Push your luck against a rival to reach the target score first.

About Dice Duel

Dice Duel is a push-your-luck strategy game. On your turn you roll a die and add the result to your turn total, then decide whether to roll again or hold and bank the points. Roll a 1 and you lose the turn total. The first player to reach the target wins.

How to play

  1. Press Roll to roll the die and add it to your turn total.
  2. Press Hold to bank your turn total into your score.
  3. A roll of 1 wipes out the turn total and ends your turn.

Tips and strategy

  • Holding at around 20 points per turn is a mathematically strong strategy.
  • Be bolder when you are far behind and safer when you are ahead.
  • Bank your points as soon as you are close enough to win.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Dice Duel?
Press Roll to roll the die and add it to your turn total. Press Hold to bank your turn total into your score. A roll of 1 wipes out the turn total and ends your turn.
What is a good strategy for Dice Duel?

How many levels does Dice Duel have?
Dice Duel has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Dice Duel is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
