Beat Runner

Hit the falling notes as they cross the line.

About Beat Runner

Beat Runner is a three-lane rhythm game. Gold notes slide down the track and you tap the matching lane button just as they cross the bright line. Notes arrive faster as levels climb, and five misses end the run. It is a clean test of timing and lane awareness.

How to play

  1. Watch the notes slide down the three lanes.
  2. Tap the lane button as a note crosses the bright line.
  3. Reach the hit target before five misses.

Tips and strategy

  • Look at the line, not at the top of the track.
  • Use three fingers on a phone, one per lane.
  • Tapping early or late both count as misses, so stay patient.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Beat Runner?
Watch the notes slide down the three lanes. Tap the lane button as a note crosses the bright line. Reach the hit target before five misses.
What is a good strategy for Beat Runner?
Look at the line, not at the top of the track.
How many levels does Beat Runner have?
Beat Runner has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Beat Runner is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
