Word Ladder

Change one letter at a time to climb from word to word.

About Word Ladder

Word Ladder shows a start word and a goal word. Each step changes exactly one letter to make a new real word. Pick the option that brings you closer to the goal. Starting words are further from the goal on higher levels, so more steps are needed.

How to play

  1. Look at the current word and the goal word.
  2. Tap the option that is one letter away and closer to the goal.
  3. Reach the goal before you run out of lives.

Tips and strategy

  • Compare letters position by position with the goal.
  • Fix the letters that already match last, not first.
  • Some one-letter changes are valid words but lead away from the goal.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Word Ladder?
Look at the current word and the goal word. Tap the option that is one letter away and closer to the goal. Reach the goal before you run out of lives.
What is a good strategy for Word Ladder?
Compare letters position by position with the goal.
How many levels does Word Ladder have?
Word Ladder has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Word Ladder is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
