Merge 2048+

Slide and merge tiles to reach the target number.

About Merge 2048+

Merge 2048+ is a sliding-tile game where matching numbers combine into larger ones. Swipe, use the arrow keys or the on-screen buttons to move all tiles. Early levels need only 128, while later ones demand the full 2048.

How to play

  1. Swipe, press an arrow key or tap an arrow button to slide every tile.
  2. Two equal tiles that collide merge into one.
  3. Reach the target tile before the board fills up.

Tips and strategy

  • Keep your biggest tile in a corner.
  • Build a row of descending values along one edge.
  • Avoid moving away from your anchor corner unless you have no other move.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How many levels does Merge 2048+ have?
Merge 2048+ has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Merge 2048+ is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
