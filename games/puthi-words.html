Puthi Words

Find the hidden Bengal-themed words in the letter grid.

About Puthi Words

Puthi Words is a word search with a Bengal flavour. Words such as river, lotus, hilsa and paddy are hidden in rows and columns of the grid. Tap the first and last letter of a word to claim it. Grids grow larger and hide more words as you progress.

How to play

  1. Look at the word list under the grid.
  2. Tap the first letter of a hidden word, then its last letter.
  3. Find every word on the list.

Tips and strategy

  • Scan for the rarest letter of a word first, such as H or J.
  • Words run left to right, right to left, top to bottom or bottom to top.
  • Cross off found words so you can see what is left.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Puthi Words?
Look at the word list under the grid. Tap the first letter of a hidden word, then its last letter. Find every word on the list.
What is a good strategy for Puthi Words?
Scan for the rarest letter of a word first, such as H or J.
How many levels does Puthi Words have?
Puthi Words has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Puthi Words is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
