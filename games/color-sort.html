Color Sort

Pour colours between tubes until each tube holds one colour.

About Color Sort

Color Sort is a relaxing pouring puzzle. Tap a tube, then tap another to pour the top colour across. You may only pour onto the same colour or into an empty tube. Sort every colour into its own tube. Two spare tubes help you.

How to play

  1. Tap a tube to pick it up, then tap where to pour.
  2. A colour can only land on the same colour or in an empty tube.
  3. Finish when every tube is empty or holds a single colour.

Tips and strategy

  • Free up an empty tube early so you always have room to manoeuvre.
  • Dig out buried colours before sorting the easy ones.
  • Check which colour sits at the bottom of each tube before you commit.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How many levels does Color Sort have?
Color Sort has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Color Sort is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
