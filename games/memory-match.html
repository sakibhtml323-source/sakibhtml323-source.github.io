Memory Match

Flip cards, remember where each symbol hides and clear the board.

About Memory Match

Memory Match trains short-term visual memory. Flip two cards at a time and remember where each symbol sits. The deck grows from four pairs up to twelve, so concentration and a good system matter more as levels progress.

How to play

  1. Tap a card to reveal its symbol.
  2. Flip a second card. If the symbols match, the pair stays open.
  3. Clear every pair with as few mistakes as possible.

Tips and strategy

  • Flip cards in a consistent order at first to build a mental map.
  • Quietly name each position, such as row 2, column 3.
  • Mistakes, not speed, decide your stars, so take your time.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How many levels does Memory Match have?
Memory Match has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Memory Match is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
