Tactic Grid

Beat the rival in a battle of four-in-a-row.

About Tactic Grid

Tactic Grid is a classic four-in-a-row strategy game against a computer rival. Drop discs into seven columns and connect four in a line before the rival does. The rival looks further ahead on higher levels, so you need to plan traps and block threats.

How to play

  1. Tap a column to drop your gold disc into it.
  2. Connect four discs horizontally, vertically or diagonally to win.
  3. Block the rival whenever it has three in a row.

Tips and strategy

  • Control the centre column: it appears in the most winning lines.
  • Try to create two threats at once so the rival can only block one.
  • Always check the rival's next move before you drop.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How many levels does Tactic Grid have?
Tactic Grid has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Tactic Grid is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
