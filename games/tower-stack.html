Tower Stack

Stack the sliding blocks as precisely as you can.

About Tower Stack

Tower Stack is a one-tap precision game. A block slides back and forth above your tower and you tap to drop it. Any overhang is cut away, so the tower gets narrower with every sloppy drop. Land blocks perfectly to keep the full width.

How to play

  1. Tap the tower or press Space to drop the sliding block.
  2. Only the part that overlaps the block below stays.
  3. Build the tower to the target height without missing completely.

Tips and strategy

  • Drop as the block crosses the centre of the tower, not at the edge.
  • Perfect drops keep your width, so aim for them.
  • Speed increases as you climb, so keep a steady rhythm.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Tower Stack?
Tap the tower or press Space to drop the sliding block. Only the part that overlaps the block below stays. Build the tower to the target height without missing completely.
What is a good strategy for Tower Stack?
Drop as the block crosses the centre of the tower, not at the edge.
How many levels does Tower Stack have?
Tower Stack has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Tower Stack is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
