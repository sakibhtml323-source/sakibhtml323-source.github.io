Knight Quest

Guide the knight to the star in as few moves as possible.

About Knight Quest

Knight Quest places a chess knight on a board with some squares blocked. Reach the star using only knight moves, within a strict move limit. Each level is generated with a known shortest route, and the target gets further away as levels advance.

How to play

  1. Tap a highlighted square to move the knight there.
  2. Knights move in an L shape: two squares one way and one square sideways.
  3. Reach the star before you use all your moves.

Tips and strategy

  • Work backwards from the star to see which squares reach it.
  • Blocked squares force detours, so plan around them.
  • Think in pairs of moves: two knight moves cover a wide area.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Knight Quest?
Tap a highlighted square to move the knight there. Knights move in an L shape: two squares one way and one square sideways. Reach the star before you use all your moves.
What is a good strategy for Knight Quest?

How many levels does Knight Quest have?
Knight Quest has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Knight Quest is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
