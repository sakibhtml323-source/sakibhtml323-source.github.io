Bubble Pop

Pop the rising bubbles before they float away.

About Bubble Pop

Bubble Pop is a calm but busy reflex game. Colourful bubbles drift up the screen and you pop them with a tap before they escape. Bubbles rise faster and appear more often as levels increase, and five escapes end the run.

How to play

  1. Tap bubbles to pop them.
  2. A bubble that floats off the top counts as an escape.
  3. Pop enough bubbles before five escape.

Tips and strategy

  • Pop the highest bubbles first.
  • Scan the whole screen, not just one spot.
  • Stay calm: speed comes with rhythm.

Levels, stars and XP

Finish a level to earn one to three stars and XP towards your player level. Replay any unlocked level to improve your stars. From level 151 the game enters endless mode, where each level is a remix of an earlier one with fresh colours and a slightly higher difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Bubble Pop?
Tap bubbles to pop them. A bubble that floats off the top counts as an escape. Pop enough bubbles before five escape.
What is a good strategy for Bubble Pop?
Pop the highest bubbles first.
How many levels does Bubble Pop have?
Bubble Pop has 150 levels that rise gradually in difficulty. After level 150 the game continues without end: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a newly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped or rotated layouts and a small difficulty boost, so the level number keeps increasing.
Is it free to play?
Yes. Bubble Pop is completely free and runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. No download or account is needed.
Does my progress save?
Your level, stars and XP are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your site data will reset them.
