Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-30

This policy explains what information AURUM ("we", "us"), available at https://sakibhtml323-source.github.io, handles when you visit the site. You can contact us at any time at sakibhtml323@gmail.com.

Summary

  • You do not need an account and we do not ask you for personal details.
  • Your game progress is saved only in your own browser.
  • We do not run analytics or tracking scripts of our own.
  • If advertising is shown, the advertising provider may use cookies or similar technology (see "Advertising").

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the site uses your browser's localStorage under keys that begin with "aurum". It stores your level in each game, stars, XP, daily-reward streak and number of wins. This data stays on your device; it is not sent to our servers. It is specific to the browser and device you use, and it is kept until you clear your site data or browser storage. If storage is blocked or corrupted the games still work, but progress will not be saved.

The site also uses a service worker and your browser cache to load pages and game files faster and to open previously visited pages offline. These hold copies of the site's own files only, and old copies are removed automatically when the site is updated.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, the hosting provider that delivers this site may keep technical logs such as IP address, browser type, requested page and time of request, for security, abuse prevention and performance. We do not control the hosting provider's retention period; it is governed by that provider's own policy.

Cookies

We do not set cookies ourselves. See our Cookie Information page.

Analytics

We do not use analytics services on this site. If that changes, this policy will be updated before it happens.

Advertising

This site may display advertising supplied by third-party advertising providers. When ads are shown, those providers may set or read cookies or similar identifiers in your browser to deliver, limit and measure ads and, depending on your region and choices, to personalise them. We do not control these technologies. The provider's own privacy policy and, where the law requires it, its consent notice explain what it does and let you choose. Advertising is never required to play the games and never affects gameplay, scores or rewards.

Third-party services

The pages and games are self-contained: they load no external fonts, scripts or trackers from us. The only third parties involved are the hosting provider that serves the site and, if enabled, the advertising provider described above.

Your choices and rights

You can delete the stored progress at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings, and you can block cookies in your browser. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK or California) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to or restrict its processing. Because we hold no account data, most requests concern data held by the hosting or advertising providers; contact us and we will help where we can.

Children

AURUM is a general-audience site and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention

Progress data lives in your browser until you remove it. We keep any e-mail you send us only as long as needed to answer you and keep a record of the correspondence.

Links to other sites

The site may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest revision. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
