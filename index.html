Why AURUM?

The collection now holds 100 original games. AURUM is built around one idea: a game should be easy to start and hard to put down. Every title loads in seconds, works on phones, tablets and desktops, and saves your progress automatically in your browser.

Levels that never run out

Each game contains 150 levels that ramp up gently. When you finish level 150 the game does not stop: levels are drawn from the first 150 in a freshly shuffled order, with new colours, flipped layouts and slightly higher difficulty. The level counter keeps climbing for as long as you like.

Rewards that matter

Complete levels to earn stars and XP, raise your player level and unlock achievements. Come back every day to extend your streak and collect bigger rewards.

Growing library

We are building a collection of 100 original games across 7 categories. New releases are added regularly, and the full catalogue shows what is playable today and what is coming next.
