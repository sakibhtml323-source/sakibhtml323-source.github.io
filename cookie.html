Cookie Information

Last updated: 2026-11-01

This page explains how AURUM uses cookies and similar technologies such as browser storage.

Cookies set by us

None. AURUM does not set cookies of its own.

Browser storage we use

The site stores your game progress (levels, stars, XP and streak) in your browser's localStorage under keys that begin with "aurum". This is needed for saving progress, stays on your device and is not shared with third parties by us. Your advertising choice (see below) is stored the same way. It also uses a service worker and cache to load files faster and work offline; they contain only the site's own files.

Third-party advertising

This site shows advertising from Adsterra (banner, native banner, social bar and pop-under formats). Adsterra and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver and measure ads and, depending on your region, to personalise them. We do not control those cookies; please read Adsterra's own policy. If your time zone suggests you are in Europe, no ad code is loaded until you choose "Show ads" in the notice at the top of the page; a Global Privacy Control signal or the choice "No ads" also keeps ad code from loading. We store only your choice, in your browser's localStorage under the key "aurum.adconsent".

Managing cookies and storage

You can view, block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings (usually under "Privacy" or "Site data"). Blocking storage does not stop the games from working, but your progress will not be remembered.
