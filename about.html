About AURUM

AURUM is a free collection of 100 browser games that start in a single tap. There is nothing to install and no account to create: open a game, play, and your progress is saved on your own device.

What you will find

The library is organised into 7 categories: puzzle, reflex, brain, strategy, arcade, casual and heritage. Every game is a separate, original piece of play with its own rules, controls and strategy, and every one follows the same promise: 150 hand-tuned levels with a gentle difficulty curve, followed by an endless mode that remixes earlier levels with new colours, flipped layouts and slightly higher difficulty so there is always something new to beat.

How the games are built

All games run entirely in your browser using standard web technology. They work with touch, mouse and keyboard, scale to the screen, and can be played in the browser's full-screen mode. Each game page explains the objective, the controls and a few strategy tips, so you can start without guessing.

Progress, stars and XP

Finishing a level earns up to three stars and experience points that raise your player level. Your streak, stars and XP are stored in your browser's local storage and never leave your device; they are specific to the browser and device you use.

Is it free?

Yes. AURUM is free for everyone. The site may show third-party advertising to help cover running costs. Ads are kept away from the game area and its controls, and gameplay never depends on them or rewards clicking them.

Our principles

  • Games load fast and work on any modern device.
  • Your progress stays on your device. We do not require accounts.
  • Each game has its own written guide: objective, controls and tips.
  • Ads, if any, never block or influence gameplay.
